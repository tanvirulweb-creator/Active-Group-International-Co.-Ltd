## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the ethos of an elite international education consultancy and language learning companion: authoritative yet accessible, energetic yet deeply reassuring. It guides aspiring students and global professionals through life-defining academic milestones, language test preparation (IELTS, PTE, TOEFL), and visa pathways.

### Aesthetic Direction: Modern Material & Tactile Clarity
The visual language merges the systematic clarity of Google Material 3 with tailored institutional refinement:
- **Clean Architectural Canvas:** Pure snow whites and delicate slate-tinted canvas backdrops establish a distraction-free environment for intensive study plans and multi-stage university applications.
- **Vibrant Growth Accents:** High-potency foliage and emerald greens signal momentum, application progress, and achievement.
- **Executive Trust Tones:** Deep midnight navy anchors academic credentials, course titles, and institutional credibility.
- **Precision Tactility:** Crisp hairline surface borders, generous touch targets conforming to Android ergonomic paradigms, and deliberate, subtle surface layering replace noisy gradients.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate sense of academic authority balanced with optimistic educational progress.

### Color Roles & Application
- **Primary (`#48A02A`):** The primary brand green. Applied to critical conversion flows, primary mobile FABs, lesson completion states, active navigation indicators, and primary call-to-action buttons.
- **Secondary (`#0B3C5D`):** Rich institutional blue. Anchors global university badges, country destination chips, official immigration status summaries, and high-level section dividers.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B`):** Warm gold accent. Dedicated strictly to scholarship alerts, priority consultation tags, exam test dates, and academic achievement accolades.
- **Neutral (`#111827`):** High-density dark neutral providing accessible typographic contrast across all screen brightness levels.

### Surface System
- Default canvas is set to `#F8FAFC`, allowing pristine white (`#FFFFFF`) cards and dialog surfaces to float crisply with subtle delineation.
- Low-intensity background tint (`#EEF4F8`) is reserved for secondary panel headers, filter toolbars, and inactive step progress rails.

## Typography

The type scale relies exclusively on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, harnessing its clean geometry, friendly apertures, and contemporary mobile legibility.

### Hierarchy & Readability
- **Display & Headlines:** Used for onboarding welcomes, test score aggregates (e.g., "IELTS Overall Band 8.0"), and major country landing pages. Dense tracking creates an executive, polished feel.
- **Titles & Labels:** Optimized for Android app bar titles, pill chips, country selectors, and bottom navigation bar labels.
- **Body & Captions:** Tuned for long-form visa criteria, university entrance prereqs, vocabulary drills, and legal consultation disclosures. Letter spacing remains neutral with loose vertical proportions to prevent eye fatigue during study sessions.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built for native Android viewports, strictly following an 8dp baseline grid with a 4dp micro-step for compact icon and badge alignments.

### Grid & Form Factors
- **Mobile Handset (< 600dp):** Single-column fluid layout with `margin: 1rem` (16dp) and `gutter: 1rem` (16dp). Cards span full width inside the content boundary.
- **Foldable & Tablet (600dp – 839dp):** Dual-pane master-detail arrangement. Application tracking list anchors left (320dp fixed), while university program requirements or interactive grammar modules expand fluidly on the right.
- **Desktop / Android Large (> 840dp):** Centered max-width container capped at 1200dp with 12 fluid columns, `2rem` outer margins, and `1.5rem` gutters.

### Rhythm Principles
- Component internal padding adheres to uniform pairs: `space-sm` vertical with `space-md` horizontal for tags and chips; `space-md` uniform padding for standard card modules.
- Vertical section groupings utilize `space-xl` (32dp) to separate distinct user intents (e.g., "Upcoming Consultations" vs. "Recommended Institutions").

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy on Android relies on structural outlines paired with ambient, tinted depth rather than heavy, harsh shadows.

### Depth Architecture
- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** `#F8FAFC` base surface without elevation or border.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Layer):** `#FFFFFF` background bound by a crisp 1px outline (`#E2E8F0`). Enhanced with an ambient drop: `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Elements & Bottom Navigation):** Raised panels and floating bottom sheets use `#FFFFFF`, a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`), and an ambient lift: `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.05)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs & Consultation Booking Pickers):** Elevated above a 40% tinted neutral backdrop (`rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.4)` backdrop blur of 4px) with high diffuse softness: `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a balanced, contemporary rounded corner language (`roundedness: 2`) that brings approachable friendliness to complex immigration forms and rigorous academic preparation tools.

### Corner Radius Mapping
- **Buttons, Text Fields, and Base Controls:** `0.5rem` (8px) for standard interactive controls, providing definite click boundaries without feeling rigid.
- **Cards, Modals, and Action Sheets:** `1rem` (16px) for major educational content cards and bottom sheet top edges.
- **Chips, Pills, and Badges:** Fully rounded (`9999px` / pill-shaped) to distinguish categorical markers and course status counters from structural cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Filled Primary:** Background `#48A02A`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `label-lg`, height 48dp (minimum touch standard). Corner radius 8px. Pressed state darkens to `#3D8B23`.
- **Secondary Outlined:** Border 1.5px `#0B3C5D`, text `#0B3C5D`, transparent background. On tap, receives 8% tint of `#0B3C5D`.
- **Floating Action Button (FAB):** 56x56dp rounded square with 16px radius, colored in brand green (`#48A02A`), icon in `#FFFFFF`, with Level 2 elevation for quick actions like "Book Advisor".

### Status Chips & Badges
- **Admission Badge:** Pill-shaped, background `#E0F2FE`, text `#0B3C5D`, font `label-sm`. Used for "Offer Letter Issued", "Visa Approved", or "Full Scholarship".
- **Application In-Review Badge:** Pill-shaped, background `#FEF3C7`, text `#92400E`, font `label-sm`.
- **Verified / Success Tag:** Pill-shaped, background `#DCFCE7`, text `#166534`, font `label-sm`.
- **Filter Chips:** 36dp height, `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#CBD5E1` border, `body-md` text. Active state uses `#0B3C5D` fill with white text.

### Text Fields & Inputs
- **Base Style:** 52dp height, `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#CBD5E1` border, 8px corner radius, `1rem` horizontal padding.
- **Focus State:** 2px border `#48A02A` with no harsh glow; floating label in `#48A02A` (`label-sm`).
- **Country / Course Selector Dropdown:** Integrated leading flag/icon slot (24dp) and trailing disclosure chevron with secondary informational subtext.

### Cards
- **University / Course Card:** White surface, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, 16px radius, 16dp internal padding. Features institutional logo at top-left, course name (`title-md`), country tag, tuition fee highlight, and admission deadline countdown badge.
- **Language Lesson Card:** Compact layout, featuring mastery progress rail (`#48A02A` progress fill against `#E2E8F0` track, 6dp height, 3dp radius), module title, and audio practice button.

### Lists & Navigation
- **Navigation Bar (Bottom):** Fixed 64dp bar, `#FFFFFF` background, top border 1px `#E2E8F0`. Four primary destinations: Consult, Universities, Test Prep, and Applications. Selected state uses `#48A02A` filled icon with active label indicator.
- **Consultation Timeline List:** Vertical step indicator connecting stages with a 2px `#E2E8F0` line (turns `#48A02A` upon step completion) and 24dp circular step markers.
